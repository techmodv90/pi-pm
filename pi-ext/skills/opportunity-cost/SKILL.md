# Opportunity Cost

Every decision has a hidden cost: the value of the best alternative not taken.

## Process

1. List all available options.
2. For each option: what do we GAIN and what do we LOSE?
3. Assess the cost of the best alternative NOT chosen.
4. Decide whether the benefit exceeds the opportunity cost.

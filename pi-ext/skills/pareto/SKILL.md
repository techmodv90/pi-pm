# Pareto (80/20)

Focus on the 20% that produces 80% of the value.

## Process

1. **List** all elements (features, bugs, tasks).
2. **Assess** the impact of each.
3. **Sort** by descending impact.
4. **Identify** the top 20%.
5. **Focus** resources on that 20%; defer the rest.

## Example

20 requested features → which 20% deliver 80% of the value?
→ Focus on ~4 high-impact features; the remaining 16 can wait.

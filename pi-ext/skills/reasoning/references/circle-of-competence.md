# Circle of Competence

Distinguish deep knowledge from superficial knowledge — and act accordingly.

## Process

1. **Define** the decision area.
2. Assess honestly: is this inside my circle of competence?
3. If YES: decide with confidence.
4. If NO: consult an expert or research before deciding — never fake depth.
5. **Document** the boundary for future decisions.

In agentic work this includes admitting "I don't know, let me check" instead of fabricating.
